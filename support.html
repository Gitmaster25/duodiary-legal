Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Assistance

1. Data Storage & iCloud Synchronization (Apple Guideline 5.1.1)

DuoDiary does not operate external proprietary databases. Your scrapbooks, photos, and journal entries are saved directly on your device and synced securely through your personal Apple iCloud container using CloudKit. Your private memories remain strictly between you and your invited partner.

2. Sharing Books & Safety Controls (Apple Guidelines 1.2 & 5.1.1)

Sharing a book is powered by Apple's CloudKit sharing infrastructure. You can invite your partner directly from within the app. You maintain complete control: you can leave a shared book or block a participant's access at any time via Book Options › Sharing safety.

3. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Apple Guideline 3.1.2)

DuoDiary Plus subscriptions and Lifetime purchases are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. You can manage or cancel your active subscription at any time in your iOS Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, you can permanently delete your DuoDiary account, CloudKit records, and local data directly within the app by navigating to Profile › Account › Delete account & data.

5. Reporting Inappropriate Content or Abuse

In every shared book, you have access to a “Sharing safety & report” action that prepares an email report. You can also contact our support team directly at duodiaryadmin@gmail.com.
