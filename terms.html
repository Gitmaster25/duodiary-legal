1. Reporting & Sharing Safety

DuoDiary provides the following safety tools:

  • In-App Reporting: In a shared book, tap Book Options › Report or Block Collaborator to submit a concern through DuoDiary's public CloudKit database for developer review. Reports include identifiers, the category, optional details, and timestamps, without automatically attaching photos, scrapbook pages, or journal entries. See the Privacy Policy for report access and retention.
  • Participant Blocking: Block a participant via Book Options › Report or Block Collaborator or Profile › Sharing safety. Blocking removes that person's access to books you own and leaves partner-owned shares involving that identity. The app shows a retry action if iCloud cleanup is unfinished. Blocking does not erase copies already saved, and unblocking does not automatically restore sharing access.
