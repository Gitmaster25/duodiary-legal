## 🛡️ Zero-Tolerance Policy (Apple Guideline 1.2)

DuoDiary enforces a strict **Zero-Tolerance Policy for objectionable content and abusive users**:
- Immediate removal of offending content and revocation of sharing access.
- Permanent termination of user accounts found to engage in harassment, hate speech, explicit content, or abuse.
- In-app abuse reporting and instant participant blocking.
